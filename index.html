About me

Building systems that scale.

I'm a Software Architect based in Brasília with deep expertise in Java, Quarkus, Spring Boot, and Angular. I design cloud-native solutions for some of Brazil's most regulated and high-traffic environments — primarily in the banking sector.

My work spans from greenfield microservices to legacy modernization, always focusing on performance, observability, and security. I hold a bachelor's in Information Systems from Estácio and a postgraduate degree in Full Stack Development from PUC-RS.

Currently at Cast Group, allocated to Banco do Brasil, leading the modernization of the card embossing platform — one of the most complex integration challenges in the project.
